# RAMAIAH INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (MSRIT)
## Autonomous Institute Affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)
### Official Academic Governance Regulations & Student Policy Handbook (2026–2027)

---

### SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE & RESIDENCE GOVERNANCE

#### 1.1.0 Statutory Minimum Attendance Requirement
Every candidate registered in an undergraduate or postgraduate degree curriculum must maintain an aggregate instructional contact attendance of not less than seventy-five percent (75%) across each registered course credit (theory, practical laboratory, and tutorial sessions independently evaluated). Candidates falling below the stipulated 75% threshold are automatically classified as 'Detained Due to Attendance Shortage' and shall be debarred from appearing in the Semester End Examinations (SEE) for the respective course.

#### 1.2.0 Condonation of Attendance Deficit on Grounds of Medical Incapacitation
The Academic Review Board, chaired by the Dean (Academic Affairs), exercises discretionary jurisdiction to condone attendance shortages of up to ten percent (10%), permitting candidates maintaining an aggregate contact percentage between 65.0% and 74.9% to register for SEE. Condonation petitions are admissible strictly under certified hospitalization or acute medical incapacitation. The petition must be accompanied by an official Medical Discharge Summary from a registered Government hospital and submitted to the Head of Department (HOD) within forty-eight (48) hours of medical convalescence.

#### 1.3.0 Prohibition of Monetary Condonation & Proxy Attendance Representation
Attendance shortages are non-compensable through financial penal remittance, administrative levies, or philanthropic donations under any statutory interpretation. Any student tendering financial inducements to faculty or administrative personnel, or procuring fraudulent biometric representation (proxy logging), shall face summary term expulsion under Malpractice Ordinance 9.2.

#### 1.4.0 Prolonged Chronic Medical Leave and Semester Break (In-Term Pause)
Where acute medical circumstances necessitate continuous absence exceeding thirty (30) instructional working days, the candidate is ineligible for standard in-term condonation. The student must petition the Academic Council for a formal 'Medical Term Suspension', permitting temporary withdrawal and re-admission at the corresponding semester cycle in the subsequent academic year with credit preservation.

### SECTION 2: ACADEMIC ACCOMMODATIONS & EXTRACURRICULAR REPRESENTATION

#### 2.1.0 Intermittent Academic Accommodations for Institutional Representation
Scholars formally deputed to represent MSRIT in inter-collegiate cultural symposiums, collegiate orchestral/band musical festivals, university-sanctioned athletic tournaments, or approved hackathon competitions are accorded duty attendance credit and compensatory assessments. Prior sanction via a Form-D Requisition must be endorsed by the Faculty Advisor and approved by the Dean of Student Welfare prior to the commencement of the scheduled event.

#### 2.2.0 Duty Leave Allocation Limits for Co-Curricular Competitions
Duty attendance accommodations under Section 2.1.0 are subject to a statutory ceiling of ten percent (10%) of total semester instructional hours. Accumulation of duty credit cannot depress actual physical classroom presence below sixty-five percent (65%).

#### 2.3.0 Compensatory Assessments for Excused Absences
Where an officially sanctioned institutional duty causes a candidate to miss a Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) test or milestone review, the department shall schedule a single compensatory internal evaluation within fourteen (14) calendar days of the student's return, provided the Form-D clearance is verified.

### SECTION 3: LABORATORY & PRACTICAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROTOCOLS

#### 3.1.0 Compensatory Makeup Laboratory Sessions
Scholars absent from regular practical laboratory sessions owing to documented medical emergencies or sanctioned extracurricular duty leave are entitled to attend a compensatory makeup laboratory cycle. Makeup sessions must be formally requisitioned with the Course Instructor and completed within ten (10) instructional working days following resumption of campus duties.

#### 3.2.0 Forfeiture of Laboratory Continuous Assessment Marks
Unexcused absence from any scheduled laboratory experiment or practical assessment shall result in immediate forfeiture of the continuous internal evaluation marks allocated to that specific instructional module, with zero entitlement to compensatory cycles.

#### 3.3.0 Laboratory Safety Compliance & Protective Gear Mandate
Failure to adhere to statutory safety regulations—including non-possession of prescribed cotton laboratory coats, protective eyewear in chemical synthesis facilities, or electrostatic discharge footwear in microelectronics centers—shall result in immediate debarment from the laboratory premise and recording of unexcused absence.

### SECTION 4: EXAMINATIONS, GRADING & EVALUATION DISPUTES

#### 4.1.0 Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) Test Rescheduling
Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) written tests are conducted in standardized institutional windows. Rescheduling of individual CIE assessments is prohibited except in cases of verified overlapping institutional representations endorsed under Section 2.1.0 or certified medical hospitalization.

#### 4.2.0 Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Challenge Valuation for CIE
A candidate disputing the objective grading of a CIE written script must submit an administrative grievance to the Department Examination Committee within three (3) working days of script inspection. Upon remittance of the statutory review fee of Rs. 300 per course script, an independent external departmental evaluator shall conduct a blind challenge valuation. If the revised score varies by $\ge 10\%$ of maximum course marks, the revised grade supersedes the initial evaluation.

#### 4.3.0 Semester End Examination (SEE) Re-Evaluation and Script Photocopy Protocol
Candidates desiring scrutiny of SEE answer scripts must apply for certified photocopies within seven (7) days of provisional grade publication. Subsequent petitions for double-blind re-evaluation require endorsement by the Course Coordinator and payment of the Controller of Examinations (COE) prescribed re-evaluation fee.

#### 4.4.0 Malpractice Penalties During Written and Practical Examinations
Possession of unauthorized physical manuscripts, programmable electronic peripherals, smart telemetry apparatus, or deceptive communication during SEE or CIE attracts immediate script confiscation, debarment from remaining examinations of the term, and minimum one-year academic suspension.

### SECTION 5: INTERNSHIPS, INDUSTRY PROJECTS & CREDIT TRANSFER

#### 5.1.0 Eighth Semester Fast-Track Full-Time Corporate Internship
Scholars in the final undergraduate year possessing a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) $\ge 7.50$ without standing academic backlogs are permitted to undertake a full-time, off-campus corporate industry internship during the eighth semester. Requisite No Objection Certification (NOC) must be obtained from the Training & Placement Cell and countersigned by the HOD.

#### 5.2.0 Credit Equivalence for Approved NPTEL / MOOC Online Certifications
Students in semesters 5 through 7 may substitute up to two open elective courses (maximum of six credits) via pre-approved SWAYAM/NPTEL or enterprise Coursera online courses, provided proctored terminal exam certificates with scores $\ge 60\%$ are submitted to the Board of Studies (BOS).

#### 5.3.0 Capstone Project Intellectual Property & Non-Disclosure Governance
All patents, technical disclosures, and commercial prototypes developed utilizing MSRIT fabrication centers, incubation seed capital, or central computational clusters remain under shared institutional intellectual property frameworks pursuant to Autonomous Institute IP Guidelines 2024.

### SECTION 6: RESIDENTIAL HOSTEL & EXTENDED CAMPUS LIFE

#### 6.1.0 Hostel Curfew Timings & Biometric Ingress Protocols
Resident scholars must complete biometric terminal ingress at the primary hostel gates prior to 21:30 hours on instructional weekdays and 22:00 hours on designated institutional holidays. Late entry requires prior written endorsement submitted by parent/guardian to the Senior Warden.

#### 6.2.0 Overnight Access Protocol for Research Laboratories and Innovation Centers
Scholars actively conducting enterprise capstone research, funded investigations, or national design hackathon fabrications may petition for extended nocturnal laboratory access up to 23:30 hours. Petitions must be endorsed by the Supervising Faculty Guide and approved by the Chief Warden.

#### 6.3.0 Residential Code on Prohibited Substances & Noise Pollution
Possession or consumption of alcoholic beverages, chemical narcotics, e-cigarettes, or prohibited incendiary substances within hostel residential zones attracts non-negotiable expulsion from the campus housing facilities and mandatory police notification where applicable.

---

### SECTION 7: TUITION LEVIES, SCHOLARSHIPS & WITHDRAWAL REFUNDS

#### 7.1.0 Institutional Fee Refund Protocol Upon Formal Course Cancellation
A student withdrawing from an enrolled program prior to the statutory commencement of institutional classes is entitled to a 100% refund of tuition levies minus an administrative processing deduction of Rs. 1,000. Withdrawals effected between 1 and 15 days post-commencement receive an 80% refund; withdrawals beyond 30 days forfeit all tuition claims.

#### 7.2.0 Merit-Cum-Means Institutional Financial Assistance Condonation
Undergraduate candidates encountering unforeseen familial economic hardship or demise of the primary earning guardian may apply to the MSRIT Welfare Endowment for deferred installment payment schemes or up to fifty percent (50%) tuition concession upon audit of verified tax declarations.

---

### SECTION 8: OMBUDSMAN, STUDENT GRIEVANCES & ANTI-RAGGING

#### 8.1.0 Zero-Tolerance Statutory Policy on Ragging and Harassment
Ragging in all forms—verbal intimidation, physical harassment, psychological duress, or digital cyberbullying—is classified as a cognizable penal offense under UGC Regulations 2009. Conviction warrants immediate expulsion, notation on permanent transcripts, and police registration under the IPC.

#### 8.2.0 Institutional Anti-Sexual Harassment Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
Any student experiencing gender-based harassment or discrimination may file a confidential redressal petition to the MSRIT Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). Inquiries commence within seven (7) days under strict confidentiality protocols.

#### 8.3.0 Equal Opportunity Accommodations for Differently Abled Scholars
Differently abled candidates possessing certified benchmark disabilities ($\ge 40\%$) are entitled to scribe facilities, twenty (20) minutes compensatory examination duration per hour, and ground-floor examination seating accommodations upon filing Form-EA with the Examination Cell.
